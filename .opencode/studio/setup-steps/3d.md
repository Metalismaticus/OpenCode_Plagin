# Step 6 — 3D

For a game in a three-dimensional world. Decide the technique yourselves and
name it in the plan with the «Решено за вас» line; ask the owner only about
what changes the feel (the camera — round (a); the hero's height — if the
reference says otherwise than an ordinary person).

## «Правила проекта» in `AGENTS.md`

Short, 3–5 lines — the reviewer judges by them:

- **Масштаб и оси:** 1 unit = 1 m, Y up (in Unreal — Z up and 1 unit = 1 cm:
  the rule in its units). The hero — <рост> m, the eyes — <высота> m
  (defaults 1.8 and 1.6); a block — <размер> m if the world is made of
  blocks.
- **Камера:** the type from round (a), the height and field of view — per the
  main reference, otherwise the engine's default.
- **Модели:** glTF/GLB; the pivot at the bottom center; in glTF the model's
  face looks toward +Z, in Godot "forward" is −Z: the rotation — in the
  import settings, not in code; collisions of imported models — with simple
  shapes (capsule, box, convex hull), not the model's mesh.
- **Бюджеты** of triangles and textures — after a measurement on the step 3
  hardware against «Бюджет производительности» of `docs/TESTING.md` (the
  player level goals, recalculated for this machine): before the measurement
  the line «?? нужно подтверждение: бюджеты — после замера»; the measurement
  itself — as a piece of the «Серая коробка» item.

## The «Настройки графики» item

A game with a 3D world — into the Stage 1 queue after «Первый кадр по
образцу», `[можно] [код]` (there is `docs/DESIGN.md` — `[ui]`, the menu via
`designer`): the expensive things from the references stay in the game, and
the game itself picks the quality for the player's computer.

- the presets низкие / средние / высокие / ультра — the steps of the
  «Лестницы качества» of the passports `docs/refs/<тема>.md`; the preset
  goals — «Бюджет производительности» of `docs/TESTING.md`;
- the ladders' main knobs — as separate sliders; the render scale (FSR and
  the like) — also a knob;
- auto-picking the preset at first launch by the hardware fingerprint via
  `tools/perf_ref.json` (Godot — `RenderingServer.get_video_adapter_name()`
  and `get_video_adapter_type()`, `OS.get_processor_count()`,
  `OS.get_memory_info()`; another engine — learn from it how to read the GPU,
  cores, and memory; the table is needed in the player build too: in Godot —
  `*.json` into the export's non-imported files filter, verify the auto-pick
  on a built version); hardware not in the table — «средние»;
- the menu: the preset and the sliders; the player's choice is saved and
  outweighs the auto-pick;
- the check: a scenario switches the preset and sees it after a restart; the
  fingerprints of the «Цели для игроков» benchmarks give their own preset.
